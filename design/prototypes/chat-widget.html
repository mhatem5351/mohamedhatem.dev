Ask AI, every state at real size

The floating assistant that answers from Hatem's CV, LinkedIn and GitHub. Each frame below is rendered by chat.js itself (static and inert), so the gallery is the component. The real widget is live on this page: use the launcher at the bottom corner, press /, or the buttons.

العربية

Panel400 × 640, radius 24, shadow-xl, 16 px above the launcher. Height min(640 px, viewport − 160 px). Phones under 640 px: full-height sheet, top radius 20.

Launcher56 px, bottom-end 24 px (16 px on phones) plus safe-area insets. Pill until the hero scrolls away, then a circle. Pages keep 96 px free at the end (--chat-clearance).

MotionOpens from the launcher corner: scale .96 → 1, rise 8 px, 380 ms spring. Reduced motion: 120 ms fade, static graph and caret.

AccessibilityNon-modal dialog on desktop, modal sheet on phones (inert page, scroll lock). Focus goes to the composer; Escape returns it to the trigger. One polite announcement per answer.

Limits4,000 characters, counter from 3,600. Composer grows to 8 lines. Pasted job descriptions collapse to 6 lines with Show more.
