Design system reference

01 · Colour

Semantic tokens

Rendered live from tokens.css. Switch theme to see the dark values. Text pairs are verified by scripts/check-contrast.mjs (220 pairs, 0 failures).

Category colours

text-safe · fill (decorative) · tint (chip background) · tint-line. Category encodes meaning: teal orchestration, saffron retrieval, coral generation, sky evaluation, sage guard, slate infra.

02 · Type

Scale, English and Arabic

Fluid between 360 and 1440 px. Arabic runs through the same tokens with --script-scale 1.0625 (text) and 0.92 (display), taller line-heights and zero tracking.
